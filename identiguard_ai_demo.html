IDENTIGUARD SHIELD

Privacy, consent & security

Privacy is a core product requirement, not an optional feature.

⬡
Data minimization

Collect only information required for a defined and lawful purpose.

▣
Encryption

Protect data in transit and at rest with managed encryption keys.

♙
Access controls

Separate citizen, institutional, officer, administrator and auditor permissions.

◷
Audit trails

Record who accessed sensitive information, when and for what approved purpose.

♡
Safeguarding

Protect children and vulnerable people from unauthorized disclosure or contact.

⌑
Human review

Do not make high-impact decisions solely from an automated biometric result.
